Pitch Bend Texture

Simple to use. Strange in the best way.

Few controls, each with one clear job, so you can find the sound by ear.

Pitch that follows the motion

The band's edges delay the sound a tiny bit. When they move, that delay changes and the pitch bends: the Doppler effect.

Four LFO shapes

Sine, Triangle, Square with soft edges (no clicks) and Random glide. Offset lets the band linger at the top or bottom.

Locks to your tempo

From 0.01 to 20 Hz, or synced from 8 bars down to 1/32, with dotted and triplet values. Locked to the bar while the song plays.

24, 48 or 96 dB/oct

Steeper edges bend more. 24 is gentle and smooth, 48 is clear and strong, 96 bends the most at slow rates.

Sweep and Band Size

Sweep sets how far the band travels. Band Size sets how wide the band itself is. The display shows both, live.

Dry/Wet turns it into a phaser

Blend in the dry signal and the moving phase cuts sweeping notches through the sound.

About SoundSo

Small tools, big movement

SoundSo is an independent audio software company. We build focused plugins that each do one thing well, and sound like nothing else.

Pitch Bend Texture is our first product. It grew out of a happy accident in a multiband saturator: when a band was dragged across the spectrum, the pitch bent. We liked it so much that it became its own plugin. More tools are on the way.
